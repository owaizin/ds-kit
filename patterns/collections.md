# Collections: records before decoration

Use when the user scans, compares and acts on multiple records. Choose table semantics when column comparison matters; a stacked list suits independently read items.

## Assemble

1. Put a title, short purpose/count and one primary collection action on a shared header rail. Secondary actions have lower visual emphasis.
2. Add summaries only if they change a useful decision. Group related totals in one strip, identify units/timeframe and use tabular numerals. The record list remains the dominant work area.
3. Place filters immediately above results, with explicit labels and the result count. Selected filters must have a recoverable clear/reset route.
4. Give record identity stronger text weight, secondary detail muted text, quantity columns end alignment and status a concise text label. Use restrained row dividers and consistent insets.
5. Keep row actions named for their record. Count/pagination describes the visible range and remains usable below the list.

## Reflow and states

On narrow screens, move secondary columns beneath identity with their meaning retained, or use a labelled local scroll region for essential comparison. Keep identity, decisive status and next action visible. Allow filters/header actions to wrap. Do not remove the only copy of an email, deadline or amount because its column is secondary.

Loading keeps region dimensions stable. “No records yet” explains creation; “No matching records” preserves filters and offers reset. Failed loading explains retry without pretending the collection is empty. Selected-row and bulk actions require a separate contract if implemented.

## Foundation mapping

Use [composition roles](../foundations/layout-composition/README.md#map-roles-rather-than-scaling-everything). Start with body-size rows, heading-weight identity, tabular values, step 4 row insets, step 8 before the collection and soft small corners on controls. Give status a subdued semantic fill/text pair; the whole row need not be tinted.

## Acceptance

Check a two-line name, long identifier, mixed statuses, zero results and enough rows to judge rhythm. At 375px retain the task-critical information and avoid document overflow. Verify filtering/reset, row action names, keyboard focus and visible outcome. Compare alignment, hierarchy and data retention with a rendered benchmark, not just token equality.
